# Business Idea

This folder collects everything needed to develop and register a business,
structured around the [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl) (Kamer van Koophandel)
approach to writing an ondernemingsplan (business plan) and starting a
business in the Netherlands.

## Structure

- `01-ondernemer/` — About you: motivation, goals, entrepreneurial skills
- `02-onderneming/` — The business idea: mission, product/service, legal form
- `03-markt/` — Market research: target audience, competition, marketing plan
- `04-financieel-plan/` — Financial plan: investment, financing, operating and cash flow budgets
- `05-kvk-registratie/` — KVK registration: business name, SBI codes, checklist
- `06-na-registratie/` — Running the business: bookkeeping, taxes, insurance, permits
