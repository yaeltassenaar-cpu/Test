# Concurrentie

## Directe concurrenten (Nederland)

| Bedrijf | Model | Doelgroep | Sterktes | Zwaktes / kansen voor ons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pligo** | Credit/token-based toy subscription, nationwide delivery (except Wadden Islands) | Children 0–6 | Established brand, nationwide coverage, also serves daycares | Opportunity: compete on price, materials transparency, or a niche age band/region |
| **RePlayClub** | Toy rental subscription incl. "luxe" and outdoor toys | Families with young children | Broad catalogue (incl. outdoor toys), professional cleaning claims | Opportunity: sharper sustainability story (material sourcing, EN 71-20 hygiene certification, CO2/waste-saved reporting) |

## Indirecte / internationale referenties
- **Whirli (UK)** — token-based toy subscription from £9.99/month, largest
  player in this space in Europe; validates the model at scale and shows a
  possible growth path (buy-to-own option after 8–12 months of rental).
- **Tweedehands speelgoed / kringloopwinkels (second-hand toy shops)** — cheaper
  but no curation, no delivery/swap convenience, no guaranteed safety/hygiene
  standard.
- **Traditional toy retailers** (bol.com, Bart Smit, Intertoys) — buying new
  is the default alternative parents compare us against on price and
  convenience.

## Onderscheidend vermogen (differentiation)
- **100% environmentally friendly materials** as a hard rule (not just a
  marketing claim) — FSC wood, organic textiles, recycled/bio-based plastics
  only, verified per SKU.
- Transparent impact reporting to subscribers (e.g. "kg of plastic avoided",
  "toys kept out of landfill") — turns sustainability into a visible,
  shareable benefit.
- Potential B2B channel (daycares) that pure-consumer competitors may
  underserve.
