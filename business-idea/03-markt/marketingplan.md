# Marketingplan

De 4 P's:

## Product
Monthly toy rental subscription, credit/token-based, exclusively
environmentally friendly materials, age-banded catalogue (0–1, 1–2, 2–4,
4–6 years), professional cleaning and EN 71 safety compliance on every item.

## Prijs
Benchmarked against direct competitors:
- Pligo/RePlayClub (NL): comparable credit-based plans.
- Whirli (UK reference): plans from £9.99/month up to £24.97/month
  (240 tokens).

Suggested starting structure (to validate with a small pilot):
- **Basic** — ~€19/month (starter credit allowance, 1 swap/month)
- **Plus** — ~€29/month (larger allowance, unlimited swaps)
- **Family/Daycare** — custom pricing for bulk/B2B

A refundable damage/loss deposit per rental period is common in this model
and should be considered to offset loss/damage risk.

## Plaats
- Direct-to-consumer via own website/webshop, nationwide delivery (courier).
- B2B channel: direct outreach to daycares/peuterspeelzalen.
- Consider a small showroom/pickup point in a starting city (e.g. Utrecht —
  central location) for local customers and photo/content shoots.

## Promotie
- **SEO/content**: target searches like "speelgoed huren", "duurzaam
  speelgoed abonnement" — both competitors already rank here, so content
  marketing (blog, comparison pages) and SEO are essential to compete.
- **Social media**: Instagram/TikTok/Pinterest aimed at parents — visual,
  before/after decluttering content, "impact" stats (plastic avoided, toys
  saved from landfill).
- **Partnerships**: parenting influencers/mom-blogs, sustainability
  communities, daycare partnerships (also a B2B sales channel).
- **Referral program**: token/credit bonuses for referring other parents
  (used successfully by Whirli).
- **Local/community**: baby fairs, playgroups, sustainability markets in
  target cities (Randstad first).
