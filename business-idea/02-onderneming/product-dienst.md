# Product / dienst

## Wat we aanbieden
A monthly toy rental subscription, structured around a credit/token system
(similar to Pligo, RePlayClub, and Whirli):

- Parents choose a monthly plan (a number of "credits" or a fixed box size).
- They select toys from an online catalogue up to their credit allowance.
- Toys are delivered to their home.
- When the child is done with a toy, it's returned (prepaid return shipping)
  and swapped for something else — no extra cost as long as they stay within
  their credit allowance.
- Every toy is cleaned, sanitized, and safety-checked before it goes to the
  next family.

## Materialen en duurzaamheid (unique selling point)
- Toys made from FSC-certified wood, organic cotton/textiles, natural rubber,
  or recycled/bio-based plastics only — no virgin single-use plastic.
- All toys comply with EU toy safety standard EN 71 (mechanical/physical
  safety, flammability, and the new EN 71-20 microbiological/hygiene
  requirements), which is a legal requirement for any toy sold or supplied in
  the EU, rental included.
- Toys designed to be cleanable (required under EN 71 for toys aimed at
  children under 36 months) — no toys that can't be sanitized between renters.
- End-of-life plan: toys that are too worn for rental are sold second-hand,
  donated, or recycled by material stream — nothing goes to landfill by
  default.

## Doelgroep per fase (leeftijdscategorieën)
Toy needs change fast in early childhood, so the catalogue and plans are
organized by age band, e.g. 0–1, 1–2, 2–4, 4–6 years — matching how
competitors (Pligo, RePlayClub) structure their offering.

## Operationele stappen
1. Toy intake & QC (safety check, EN 71 compliance, photograph, catalogue).
2. Cleaning/sanitizing protocol between every rental.
3. Warehousing & inventory tracking (per-item rental history, condition,
   depreciation).
4. Pick, pack, and ship to customer; prepaid return label included.
5. Inspection on return; repair, restock, or retire the toy.
